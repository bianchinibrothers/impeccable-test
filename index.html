What it keeps

Four kinds of record, each joined to your child rather than scattered across a pediatrician, a school portal, and your inbox.

01 / HEALTH
Immunizations & health
Doses with dates, allergies, and conditions, plus what's due next against the standard childhood schedule. A record you can read off a form, not medical advice.
02 / SCHOOL
Education
Schools, terms, teachers, and the milestones and reports worth keeping. The answer to "where were they in 2023" without digging through email.
03 / KITH
The people they love
The friends your child holds close, those friends' parents, and how to reach them. Kept like they matter, because to your child they do.
04 / PREF
Preferences & needs
Foods, fears, comforts, routines. The two-minute brief that lets a grandparent or a new sitter get the evening right.

When you'll reach for it

The record is quiet for months, then a moment asks for exactly what's in it.

A / FORM
An enrolment form
It wants the date of the last tetanus shot, every prior school, two emergency contacts. You have them, in order.
B / SITTER
A new sitter
Hand them the preferences page. Allergies, bedtime, the song. Two minutes instead of twenty, and nothing forgotten.
C / CARE
Urgent care
The triage nurse asks what they're allergic to and when they last had a booster. You read it out; you're not guessing.
D / HOMES
Two households
Both caregivers keep one record. Invite per child, choose read or contribute, revoke access any time.
